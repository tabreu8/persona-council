# Proposal: new hiring loop for senior engineers

**Owner:** Engineering leadership

## Problem

Our loop takes 4.5 weeks from first contact to offer. We lose candidates to
faster-moving companies. Two of our last five preferred candidates accepted
elsewhere while waiting on our final round.

## Proposed loop

1. Recruiter screen (30 min)
2. Take-home exercise (candidates report 4-6 hours)
3. Technical deep-dive on the take-home (60 min)
4. Team fit conversation with 3 engineers (45 min)
5. Offer

Target: 10 days end to end.

## Changes from today

- Removes the second system-design interview
- Take-home replaces the live coding round
- Team fit round becomes a majority vote among the three engineers

## Rollout

Applies to all senior engineering roles starting immediately.
