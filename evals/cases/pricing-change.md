# Proposal: move to usage-based pricing

**Author:** Growth team · **Status:** for decision

## Summary

We move all customers from per-seat ($40/seat/month) to usage-based pricing
($0.008 per API call) at the start of next quarter. Billing switches on the 1st
for everyone at once.

## Rationale

Our top 15% of accounts by usage generate 61% of infrastructure cost but only
22% of revenue. Per-seat pricing rewards exactly the wrong behaviour: customers
buy few seats and hammer the API. Three competitors have moved to usage pricing
in the past 18 months.

## Modelling

Applying the new rate card to a sample of 30 accounts from March, revenue would
have been 14% higher. Extrapolated across the base, that is roughly $2.1M ARR.

## Plan

- Week 1: announce by email
- Week 2: update pricing page and docs
- Week 4: new rate card goes live for all accounts
- No change to contracts; our ToS permits pricing changes with 14 days notice
