# Launch plan: "Instant Insights"

**Owner:** Marketing · **Launch date:** in 3 weeks

## Positioning

"Instant Insights turns any spreadsheet into a board-ready analysis in seconds.
No analysts. No waiting. No mistakes."

## Channels

- Launch post on our blog, cross-posted to LinkedIn
- Email to the full list (46k contacts)
- Paid: $30k across search and LinkedIn, front-loaded into week one
- Outreach to 12 journalists who covered our Series A

## Proof points

- "10x faster than a human analyst" (internal benchmark, 20 test files)
- "Trusted by teams at [three logos from our design partner programme]"
- Free tier, no credit card

## Success metric

5,000 signups in the first month.

## Risks

- Competitor may announce something similar. Mitigation: launch first.
